GTA VI trophy guide and tracker — all 55 PS5 trophies with their official names, descriptions and grades, a guide in our own words for every trophy with its sources cited, a roadmap to the Platinum, and a checklist that saves progress in the browser. Works fully offline; no account, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Hand it in with `node main.ts` (writes `dossier.json`, `app/` and `screenshots/` to `/output`, or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
